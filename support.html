Common questions

Why didn't a bell arrive at the same time as before?

Times are chosen randomly and refreshed when you return to the app. If you do not open Here, the weekly pattern repeats. Here never reveals the next exact time.

How do I turn reminders off or pause them?

On the Home screen you can pause reminders; they stay paused until you resume. In Settings you can switch reminders off entirely or edit your schedule.

I denied notifications by mistake.

Journalling still works without notifications. To allow reminders, open Settings inside Here and follow the link to system settings, then enable notifications for Here.

How do I keep a copy of my moments?

Go to Settings → Your data and export as JSON or plain text. Exported files leave through the system share sheet. Your journal is not included in device or cloud backups in this release.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Your data → Erase all data. This removes your moments, drafts, reminders and settings. Previously exported copies cannot be erased by the app.
