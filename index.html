Here — Privacy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Here is a calm app that sends a gentle bell at unpredictable times with a short presence prompt. This page explains how the app handles your information. It is written to match how the app actually works.

Where your data lives

Here has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your moments are stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they return automatically when you sign in on a new iPhone. That data belongs to your Apple ID and is governed by Apple's terms; the developer of Here does not see or receive it, and it is never sent to any third party. Your reminder settings, drafts and reminder history stay only on the device.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

Here contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. There is no tracking, no advertising identifier and no profile of your use. Mood data is never sent to any analytics service.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device. Notification content is generated on-device. If you choose generic notification wording, the notification shows a neutral message instead of the prompt.

Sync and export

Because your moments live in your private iCloud, they reappear when you sign in on another iPhone with the same Apple ID. If you prefer a local copy, you can export your journal or a JSON backup from Settings; exported files leave the app through the system share sheet and are under your control once shared. If you are not signed into iCloud, Here still works and stores your moments on the device.

App lock

You may optionally protect Here with your device passcode or biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID). This uses Apple's system authentication. Here never receives or stores your biometric data.

Children

Here is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
